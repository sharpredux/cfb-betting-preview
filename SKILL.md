# CFB Betting Preview

## Resolve the schedule

- Cover the named matchup only. Resolve its next scheduled meeting if no date was given. A single named team selects its next eligible game. Ask for clarification when names identify multiple schools.
- Without a named game or team, default to the full requested day, or today if no date was provided. Display times and delimit each slate by the user’s timezone, defaulting to Asia/Manila. Use official season labels for a numbered week (including Week 0), conference championship, named bowl, or CFP round. Cover the full requested week or round across local dates.
- Use [the research guide](references/research.md) to confirm venue, kickoff or tip, season, and status. Include postponed, canceled, live, completed, or provisional games where they belong to the requested scope. Reserve pregame calls for games with confirmed teams and start times that are still upcoming. If the slate is empty, say so for the requested window.
- Slate scans and team-only searches require two eligible teams. When both teams are explicitly named and at least one was FBS that season, preview the mixed matchup with current sources. Persist profiles for eligible participants only. Verify historical membership against official sources for the requested season.

## Research and assess markets

- Follow [the team profile workflow](references/team-profiles.md) for the eligible teams in the requested games. Reuse a matching-season profile verified less than 15 minutes ago. Refresh due profiles locally as evidence is verified. Share profile updates only with authorization.
- Compare quarterback and line availability, protection and pressure, pace and explosiveness, opponent quality, rest and travel, weather and roof, and postseason opt-outs or coaching changes. Check live availability and odds for each requested game independently of profile freshness. For historical analysis use evidence available before the start and keep profile workflows read-only.
- Compare full-game spread, total, and moneyline using [the market guide](references/edges.md). Use user-supplied lines or the requested sportsbook first. Verify the price and its observation time, and evaluate each market independently. Keep props outside the preview unless requested.

## Present the preview

- For a matchup, lead with the verified start, status, venue, material availability, rest and travel, recent form, and all three quotes. Give a short assessment for each market and the best supported angle or **Pass**. Include the strongest counterargument and a concrete news or price trigger that would change the call.
- For a slate, show every eligible game in compact chronological rows with status and market assessments. Expand qualifying angles and material uncertainties. Use **Pass / Lean / Moderate / Strong**, with Leans as watchlist items. Cite direct sources and timestamp time-sensitive information.
